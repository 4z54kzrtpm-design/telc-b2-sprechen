15-minute routine

A simple daily routine for exam speaking practice.

1. Prepare — 3 min
Read the task. Write only keywords, not full sentences.
2. Speak — 4 min
Speak freely. Use your structure and connect your ideas.
3. Record — 4 min
Listen once. Check fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation.
4. Repeat — 4 min
Answer the same task again without looking at the model answer.
Tip: The goal is not to memorize one perfect answer. Build reusable speaking structures.
